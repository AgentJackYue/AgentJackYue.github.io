## Selected Experience

### RoboMaster — Robot Control and System Integration

As an electrical control lead for the infantry robot team at ShanghaiTech University, I worked on robot control and system integration, including chassis kinematics, gimbal closed-loop control, CAN communication, and PCB design.

This experience strengthened my understanding of the interaction between software, electronics, and physical systems, as well as the importance of reliable control in real-world environments.

### Quadrotor Modeling and Model Predictive Control

During a research internship at MAgICLab, I worked on quadrotor dynamics modeling and model predictive control. My work involved exploring system dynamics and control strategies for autonomous flight.

This experience motivated me to further explore the relationship between mathematical modeling, optimization, and robotic autonomy.

### Image Processing

I have also worked on image processing tasks involving defect detection and stripe-region restoration. This work introduced me to practical challenges in image reconstruction, including edge preservation and the recovery of local image structure.
